---

### Slide 2: The Lunar South Pole Problem (0:15 – 0:35)
"Landing at the Lunar South Pole is an extraordinary engineering challenge. Because the Moon's axial tilt is just 1.54 degrees, the Sun grazes the horizon in a perpetual 360-degree circle. Crater walls cast shadows tens of kilometers long, dropping temperatures by 150 degrees in seconds. Simultaneously, lunar optical libration causes Earth to wobble and dip behind crater rims, severing direct radio links. Existing tools are complex desktop programs that take hours to set up."

---

### Slide 3: Our Solution (0:35 – 0:55)
"SeleneSight solves this in the browser in under two seconds. We provide an interactive 360-degree Polar Dome skyplot that displays solar and Earth trajectories against true LOLA terrain horizon masks. We model a full 708-hour synodic day power curve, and feature an instant Side-by-Side Scenario Comparator to evaluate alternative landing sites and dates."

---

### Slide 4: User Experience & Telemetry (0:55 – 1:15)
"In three clicks, a mission planner selects a landing site—like Shackleton Rim or Mons Mouton—computes the true crater-rim horizon shadow mask, and isolates simultaneous 'Golden Windows' of sunlight and comms. Live countdown clocks track exact hours until the next sunset, sunrise, earthset, or earthrise, with one-click CSV and JSON export for mission teams."

---

### Slide 5: Scientific Validity & Space Agency Data (1:15 – 1:35)
"SeleneSight is grounded in open space agency data: NASA LOLA digital elevation models for 360-degree terrain horizon profiling, JPL Horizons for orbital vectors, and LROC QuickMap and Moon Trek for site verification. We also incorporate Canadian Space Agency operational contexts for Canadarm3 and Canadian lunar rover power constraints."

---

### Slide 6: Dual Accessibility (1:35 – 1:50)
"SeleneSight serves dual audiences: an Aerospace Telemetry mode for mission directors and engineers, and an Educator Mode with rich, plain-language science cards that explain orbital mechanics, cold traps, and robotics to students and the public—all with zero logins and zero installations."

---

### Slide 7: Vision & Future Impact (1:50 – 2:05)
"As humanity prepares to return to the Moon, SeleneSight demonstrates that high-precision planetary science tools can be accessible, rapid, and open to all. You can explore our live application and open-source code today. Thank you!"
