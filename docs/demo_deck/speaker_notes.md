### Slide 2: The Lunar South Pole Problem (0:15 – 0:35)
"Operating at the Lunar South Pole presents two critical physical barriers:
First, because the Moon's axial tilt is only 1.54 degrees, the Sun grazes the horizon in a 360-degree circle. Horizontal solar panels produce under 3% efficiency, while crater rims cast 20-kilometer shadows that cause instant 150°C temperature plunges, freezing rover batteries.
Second, lunar optical libration causes Earth to wag and nod across the horizon, repeatedly dropping behind towering massifs and cutting Direct-to-Earth telemetry. Existing SPICE and desktop GIS tools take hours to configure, creating an operational bottleneck for rapid mission planning."

---

### Slide 3: Our Solution — SeleneSight Core Innovations (0:35 – 0:55)
"SeleneSight solves this directly in any browser in under two seconds:
1. An interactive **360-degree Polar Dome Skyplot** displaying the gold solar trajectory and cyan Earth libration loop directly against the red LOLA terrain horizon mask.
2. A continuous **708-hour Synodic Ribbon Timeline** calculating hourly power curves for vertical masts versus two-axis tracking arrays.
3. An instant **Side-by-Side Scenario Comparator** computing exact trade-off deltas between candidate landing sites or launch dates across six mission metrics.
All paired with live predictive countdown clocks to the next sunset, sunrise, earthset, and earthrise."

### Slide 4: Real-World Mission Workflow (0:55 – 1:15)

2. Calculate the 360-degree LOLA topographic horizon mask with vacuum dip allowance.
3. Pinpoint simultaneous 'Golden Windows' of active solar generation and direct line-of-sight communications.
4. Export JPL Horizons-compatible 710-row CSV or JSON ephemerides, or launch one-click deep links directly into NASA LROC QuickMap, Moon Trek, and CSA portals."
