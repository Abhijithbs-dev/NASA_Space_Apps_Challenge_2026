THE PROBLEM

The Lunar South Pole Operational Bottleneck

☀️ Extreme Grazing Sunlight

The Moon's 1.54° axial tilt keeps the Sun skimming the horizon in a 360° circle. Horizontal solar panels yield under 3% efficiency. Even modest crater rims cast 20-km shadows.

📡 Libration Radio Blackouts

Optical libration (±6.68° lat, ±7.90° lon) causes Earth to nod up and down, dropping below jagged massifs and severing Direct-to-Earth (DTE) communications.

Existing desktop mission tools (SPICE kernels, desktop GIS) are fragmented, complex, and inaccessible to field planners and rapid mission evaluators.
