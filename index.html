Integrated Planetary Resources & Space Agency Standards

SeleneSight links directly with official planetary data systems from NASA, JPL, and the Canadian Space Agency. All topocentric elevation algorithms are verified against LOLA digital elevation models and JPL Horizons ephemeris outputs.

NASA LROC QuickMap LRO / LOLA

Interactive planetary GIS hosting Narrow Angle Camera (NAC) mosaics, LOLA topography, and slope hazard distributions for all 12 candidate Artemis polar sites.

Open Active Site in LROC QuickMap →

NASA Moon Trek JPL / NASA

Coordinate-anchored 3D terrain viewer providing digital elevation profiles, crater rim crest elevations, and rover traverse route planning.

Open Active Site in NASA Moon Trek →

JPL Horizons On-Line Ephemeris NASA SSD

Solar System Dynamics benchmark verifying sub-solar drift (0.5079°/hr), 1 AU solar irradiance (1,361.0 W/m²), and optical libration amplitudes (±6.68° lat, ±7.90° lon).

Access JPL Horizons Web App →

Canadian Space Agency (CSA) Canadarm3 / LUV

Operational partner modeling lunar south pole power requirements for the Canadian Lunar Utility Rover and Gateway NRHO communication passes serviced by Canadarm3.
